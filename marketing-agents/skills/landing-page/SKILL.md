# Landing page

## Method

Match page intent to the traffic source and funnel stage. Structure the page around audience, problem, promise, proof, objections and one primary next step. Use supplied research and brand constraints; propose tests only when traffic supports them.

## Deliverable

A page outline and ready-to-edit copy for headline, subhead, proof, benefits, objections, CTA, form and confirmation state.

## Review

Keep claims verifiable and forms proportionate. Do not fabricate reviews, awards, scarcity or conversion results.
