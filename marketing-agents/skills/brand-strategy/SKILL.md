# Brand strategy

## Method

Audit the audience, category, promise, proof and existing brand signals. Separate customer language from internal assumptions. Identify a focused brand opportunity, the strategic trade-offs it implies, and evidence needed to validate it.

## Deliverable

A one-page brand strategy: audience, category frame, core promise, reasons to believe, voice principles, exclusions and three validation actions.

## Review

Do not claim distinctiveness without competitor and customer evidence. Keep the promise specific, supportable and consistent with the product.
