# Campaign brief

## Method

Translate the requested outcome into one audience, behavior change and offer. Specify why now, channel roles, deliverables, budget ceiling, timing, owners, dependencies and measurement before execution.

## Deliverable

A review-ready campaign brief with goal, audience, insight, promise, offer, channels, assets, timeline, budget, owners, risks and approval checkpoints.

## Review

Resolve conflicting objectives. Mark unknowns and assumptions, and ensure claims and approvals are feasible before production.
