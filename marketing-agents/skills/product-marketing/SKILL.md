# Product positioning

## Method

Identify the buyer, the buying trigger, the alternative they use today and one advantage the company can support.

## Deliverable

Write a positioning brief with one promise, its evidence and a segment to exclude.

## Review

Flag assumptions about buyers separately from observed customer language.
