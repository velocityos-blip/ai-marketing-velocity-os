# Affiliate marketing

## Method

Assess whether partner-led acquisition fits the offer, margin and customer journey. Define partner qualification, economics, tracking, enablement, disclosures, fraud controls and a limited pilot before scaling.

## Deliverable

An affiliate pilot plan with eligibility, commission assumptions, attribution window, assets, disclosure guidance, quality checks and review thresholds.

## Review

Do not promise earnings or make partners' claims for them. Require clear disclosures, brand rules and validation of incremental, qualified outcomes.
