# Customer marketing

## Method

Identify customer segments and moments where participation benefits both the customer and the business. Design advocacy, education or community programs with clear value exchange, consent and accessible participation options.

## Deliverable

A customer program brief with segment, value to customer, activities, recruitment, enablement, owner, success measures and feedback loop.

## Review

Do not assume advocacy consent from purchase or employment. Get explicit approval for quotes, logos, case studies and public participation.
