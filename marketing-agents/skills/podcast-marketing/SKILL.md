# Podcast marketing

## Method

Set a distinct listener promise and repeatable editorial format. Plan episode topics, guest fit, preparation, recording, editing, distribution and repurposing from available resources and audience evidence.

## Deliverable

A podcast concept and episode plan with audience, format, episode brief, guest outreach outline, production checklist and distribution measures.

## Review

Get guest consent for recording and reuse. Verify facts and disclose sponsorships or material relationships.
