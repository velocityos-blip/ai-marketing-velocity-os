# Messaging framework

## Method

Turn the approved positioning into audience-specific message layers. Build a core narrative, value pillars, proof points and objection responses. Adapt phrasing to funnel stage without changing the underlying promise.

## Deliverable

A concise messaging matrix with audience, pain, message, proof, objection and CTA, plus a short elevator pitch.

## Review

Use customer vocabulary where available. Separate verified facts from proposed claims and do not invent testimonials, metrics or customer outcomes.
