# Copy editing

## Method

Read supplied copy against the intended audience, claim evidence and desired action. Remove ambiguity while preserving meaning.

## Deliverable

Return revised copy and a short account of material changes.

## Review

Verify that prices, promises and factual qualifications survive the edit.
