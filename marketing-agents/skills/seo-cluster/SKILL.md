# Search topic clusters

## Method

Group queries by buyer task and overlapping intent. Assign a primary URL to each group and link supporting pages to a hub. Flag competing URLs.

## Deliverable

A cluster map with page ownership, missing pages and internal links.

## Review

Do not merge two queries solely because they share words.
