# Positioning

## Method

Clarify target segment, urgent problem, current alternatives and purchase criteria. Compare the product against direct and status-quo alternatives. Draft several positioning options, then score each for relevance, credibility, differentiation and ease of proof.

## Deliverable

A positioning brief with target, problem, category, value, differentiation, proof points, alternatives and the recommended statement.

## Review

Avoid generic category adjectives and unsupported superiority claims. Flag any positioning that depends on an unverified capability.
