# Churn prevention

## Method

Segment departures by lifecycle stage and reason. Distinguish product friction from price, fit and involuntary churn.

## Deliverable

Propose targeted interventions and a retention cohort comparison.

## Review

Measure durable retention and preserve a clear cancellation path.
