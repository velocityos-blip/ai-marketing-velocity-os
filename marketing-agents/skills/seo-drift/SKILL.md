# SEO change detection

## Method

Match before and after records by normalized URL. Compare status, title, canonical, indexability and sitemap presence. Group regressions by shared template.

## Deliverable

A change log separating new, resolved and persistent issues with retest priorities.

## Review

Record capture dates and exclude URLs missing solely because sample scope changed.
