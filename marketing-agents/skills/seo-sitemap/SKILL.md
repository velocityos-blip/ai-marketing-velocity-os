# XML sitemap coverage

## Method

Inspect supplied sitemap records, compare listed URLs with preferred indexable pages, and flag duplicates, redirects and questionable last-modified dates.

## Deliverable

A sitemap reconciliation with removals, additions and split suggestions.

## Review

A sitemap listing indicates discovery intent only. Verify indexing separately.
