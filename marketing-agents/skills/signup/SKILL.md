# Signup optimization

## Method

Inspect each required field and error path. Identify where a visitor abandons the flow and what information can be deferred.

## Deliverable

Propose a revised signup flow and an instrumented completion test.

## Review

Preserve security requirements and measure qualified activation after signup.
