# Conversion optimization

## Method

Locate the largest measured funnel drop. Form a hypothesis about information, trust or interaction friction at that step.

## Deliverable

Prioritize one change and define its conversion and quality measures.

## Review

Check whether improved conversion also preserves downstream lead quality.
