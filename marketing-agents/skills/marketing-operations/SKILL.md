# Marketing operations

## Method

Map the campaign process, data flow, tools, ownership and failure points. Prioritize reliable instrumentation, naming, lifecycle handoffs and governance before adding automation. Make dependencies and access needs explicit.

## Deliverable

A workflow map and prioritized ops backlog with source of truth, fields/events, owners, QA steps, permissions and rollout plan.

## Review

Collect only necessary personal data. Preserve access controls, consent status, retention rules and a manual recovery path.
