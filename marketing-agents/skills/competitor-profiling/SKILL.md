# Competitor profiling

## Method

Compare positioning, ideal buyer, pricing and proof using supplied public material. Date each observation.

## Deliverable

Produce a competitor dossier with substantiated differences and open questions.

## Review

Do not present inferred revenue, traction or customer counts as facts.
