# Newsletter marketing

## Method

Define the reader promise and editorial cadence. Select useful recurring sections based on audience needs, then plan acquisition, consent, deliverability, content production and measurement.

## Deliverable

A newsletter brief or issue draft with subject options, preview text, section outline, CTA, cadence and measurement checklist.

## Review

Use permission-based lists, identify the sender, provide a working unsubscribe path and avoid misleading subject lines.
