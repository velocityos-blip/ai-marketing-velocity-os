# Customer success marketing

## Method

Use the customer journey and product adoption evidence to identify an achievable behavior change. Align messages with customer success milestones and coordinate ownership, timing and account context.

## Deliverable

An adoption campaign plan with eligible cohorts, desired behavior, education assets, CS handoff, measurement and exclusions.

## Review

Never imply that marketing outreach replaces account-specific support. Respect account communication preferences and exclude unresolved sensitive cases when appropriate.
