# Product-led growth

## Method

Trace the path from acquisition to first value, repeated value and expansion. Find the largest observed friction using supplied product data or user feedback. Propose a small experiment that improves one activation or retention behavior.

## Deliverable

A user journey, prioritized friction points, experiment card and measurement plan covering exposure, activation, guardrails and decision threshold.

## Review

Do not infer user intent from aggregate counts alone. Distinguish correlation from causation and protect product quality and user consent.
