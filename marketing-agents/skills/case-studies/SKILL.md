# Customer case studies

## Method

Plan a customer-approved story around the initial challenge, decision, implementation and evidenced outcome. Prepare neutral interview questions and a fact-check workflow. Separate customer statements from company narrative.

## Deliverable

A case-study outline or draft with approval markers, evidence needed, customer quote placeholders and a concise proof summary.

## Review

Never invent quotes or results. Obtain written approval for names, logos, metrics and publication, and honor agreed redactions.
