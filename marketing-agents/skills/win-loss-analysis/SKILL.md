# Win-loss analysis

## Method

Define the deal cohort and decision period. Separate closed-won and closed-lost outcomes, then code evidence across problem, alternatives, buying process, product fit, price and trust. Distinguish interview findings from CRM fields and anecdote.

## Deliverable

A findings memo with sample definition, themes, representative paraphrases, confidence, segment differences and three prioritized actions.

## Review

Do not overgeneralize from a small or biased sample. Remove personal data and obtain permission before quoting interviewees.
