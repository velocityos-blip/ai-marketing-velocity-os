# Marketing planning

## Method

Work backward from the goal and deadline. Estimate required conversions at each stage, then allocate effort to the weakest measured stage.

## Deliverable

Deliver a dated plan with owners, budget ceilings and a weekly decision point.

## Review

Label unknown conversion rates and show how each will be measured.
