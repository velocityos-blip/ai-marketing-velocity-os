# Answer-engine page optimization

## Method

Review the supplied page, its target audience and query, and any available analytics or search evidence. Identify whether the page answers the primary task clearly, states definitions and scope, distinguishes facts from opinion, and supplies original proof or useful examples.

Recommend edits that make the page easier for people and retrieval systems to understand: descriptive headings, a direct answer where appropriate, specific terminology, short navigable sections, accessible text for important information, accurate links, clear author or organization context, and visible publication or update details when they are maintained. Preserve nuance, limitations and necessary context.

Check that structured data, titles, descriptions, and visible content agree. Use schema only when it accurately describes the page and is supported by the relevant platform's documentation. Google states that its AI search features have no special schema or AI text-file requirement; do not prescribe llms.txt, FAQ markup, or answer blocks as guaranteed ranking or citation signals.

## Deliverable

Provide a prioritized edit plan with:

- Page URL or supplied page identifier, audience and intended query/task.
- Evidence-backed strengths and gaps, quoting or pointing to the relevant page sections.
- Suggested title, heading structure, concise answer, and revised copy where useful.
- Claims that need sources, expert review, original evidence, or correction.
- Technical and internal-link issues to verify, with owner and retest.
- A short change log separating editorial recommendations from verified technical defects.

## Review

Do not invent credentials, statistics, testimonials, citations, or freshness dates. Keep necessary qualifications next to claims. Mark recommendations as hypotheses until tested; do not promise AI Overview, answer-engine, or generative search inclusion.
