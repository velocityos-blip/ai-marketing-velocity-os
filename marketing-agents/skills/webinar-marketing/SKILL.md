# Webinar marketing

## Method

Define the audience problem and practical learning outcome. Build a program, speaker plan, registration path, reminder sequence, live engagement, recording follow-up and sales or nurture handoff.

## Deliverable

A webinar campaign kit with title, abstract, run of show, promotion timeline, emails, CTA and attendance-to-next-step measurement.

## Review

Confirm speaker permissions, recording consent, accessibility, time zones and truthful promotional claims.
