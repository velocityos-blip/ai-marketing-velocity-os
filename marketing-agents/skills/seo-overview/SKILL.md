# SEO assessment and triage

## Method

Identify the business outcome, site type and scope. Inventory evidence across discovery, indexing, page usefulness and conversion. Route each uncertainty to the narrowest specialist.

## Deliverable

A short assessment with the next three investigations, their owners and the evidence each needs.

## Review

Do not assign a site health score to an unobserved site.
