# Go-to-market strategy

## Method

Coordinate target segment, positioning, packaging, pricing inputs, launch readiness, channel roles, sales enablement, customer support and measurement. Sequence work around dependencies and readiness gates.

## Deliverable

A GTM plan with audience, value proposition, launch tier, workstreams, owners, timeline, readiness checklist and post-launch decisions.

## Review

Do not claim readiness without named owners and evidence. Mark legal, privacy, localization and support dependencies for review.
