# Generative engine visibility audit

## Method

Define the brand, products, competitors, audience, market, locale, and buying questions. Build a stable prompt panel from actual customer language and relevant informational, comparison, and decision tasks. Avoid prompts designed only to trigger a preferred answer.

Run the same prompts on named AI search surfaces and record date, locale, product/version when visible, exact prompt, answer, citations, and links. Use supplied captures or available browsing. If a surface cannot be checked, mark it unavailable; do not fabricate responses. Repeat important prompts because answers and citations vary.

For each answer, assess whether the brand is mentioned, accurately described, compared, or cited; whether a cited owned URL supports the claim; and which third-party sources are cited instead. Note factual errors, stale claims, source gaps, and opportunities for stronger first-party evidence or credible external coverage. Do not treat model output as proof of market demand or engine ranking.

## Deliverable

Provide:

- Scope and reproducible prompt panel by audience task, market and engine.
- Result log with date, exact prompt, brand outcome, cited URLs, and source quality.
- Coverage summary: mention and citation rates with denominators and sample limits.
- Priority gaps by page, factual claim, owned evidence, technical access, or external source.
- Recommendations with owner, effort, evidence needed, and a retest date.

## Review

Keep raw observations separate from interpretation. Do not generalize from one prompt or one run, promise citation gains, or recommend manufactured reviews, fake citations, or undisclosed paid placements.
