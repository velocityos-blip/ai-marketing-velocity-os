# Campaign teardown

## Method

Trace a visible campaign from first touch to conversion. Distinguish observed creative and distribution from hypotheses about targeting.

## Deliverable

Map the funnel and propose one transferable experiment for this company.

## Review

Do not infer private spend or performance from public visibility.
