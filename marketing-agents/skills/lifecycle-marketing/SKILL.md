# Lifecycle marketing

## Method

Map meaningful customer states and events from signup through adoption, renewal and reactivation. For each state, choose one helpful message, trigger, channel, timing and suppression condition. Keep frequency and consent rules explicit.

## Deliverable

A lifecycle map and sequence table with segment, trigger, message purpose, CTA, delay, exit condition and success metric.

## Review

Avoid sending messages without a lawful basis or applicable consent. Include suppression, frequency caps and an easy unsubscribe path.
