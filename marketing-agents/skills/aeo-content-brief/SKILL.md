# Answer-engine content brief

## Method

Start from a defined audience, product or subject, market, and business goal. Collect the real questions people ask from supplied Search Console, site search, support, sales, community, and search-result evidence. Group questions by task and intent; do not invent volume or demand data.

For each priority question, identify the answer the page should give, what must be true for that answer to be reliable, and what first-party evidence or qualified source supports it. Separate known facts, assumptions, and research still needed. Plan one useful page per distinct reader task and show how it connects to existing pages.

Write for people first. Use a direct response near the relevant heading, then provide context, steps, caveats, examples, and sources. A concise answer can help readers, but no layout guarantees selection or citation by an answer engine. Do not recommend keyword stuffing, fabricated FAQs, or machine-readable files as a shortcut.

## Deliverable

Return a writer-ready brief containing:

- Audience, market, conversion goal, and primary reader task.
- Prioritized question cluster with intent and evidence source for each question.
- Proposed page title, URL suggestion, direct-answer summary, and heading outline.
- Facts and first-party proof to include, sources to cite, and claims requiring verification.
- Internal links, relevant visuals or data, CTA, and content owner.
- Update triggers and open questions.

## Review

Confirm that each proposed answer is useful without the sales pitch, supported by evidence, and scoped to the reader's question. Label missing research clearly. Do not present estimated search demand, engine behavior, or rankings as facts unless the supplied data supports them.
