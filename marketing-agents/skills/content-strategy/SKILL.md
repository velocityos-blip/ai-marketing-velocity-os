# Content strategy

## Method

Map buyer questions to funnel stages. Group topics by intent and prioritize gaps with evidence of demand.

## Deliverable

Build an editorial calendar with purpose, distribution and success metric per item.

## Review

Avoid duplicate intent and assign each item a measurable job.
