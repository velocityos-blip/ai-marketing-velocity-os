# Public relations

## Method

Find a verifiable news angle and match it to a publication's audience. Separate announcement facts from company opinion.

## Deliverable

Write a pitch, supporting fact sheet and follow-up plan.

## Review

Check dates, quotes and evidence before recommending outreach.
