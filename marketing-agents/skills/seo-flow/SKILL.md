# Evidence-led SEO experiments

## Method

Find an opportunity in supplied search data, identify an existing distribution advantage, choose one page change and follow the result through to a qualified business outcome.

## Deliverable

An experiment brief with baseline, intervention, comparison window and decision rule.

## Review

State which stage lacks evidence and what observation would change the recommendation.
