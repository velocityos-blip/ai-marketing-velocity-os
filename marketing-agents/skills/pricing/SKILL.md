# Pricing strategy

## Method

Identify the unit of value and customer segments. Compare packages against willingness-to-pay evidence and service cost.

## Deliverable

Propose a packaging matrix and a reversible price test.

## Review

Show assumptions, margin risks and impacts on existing customers.
