# Offer design

## Method

Map the buyer's desired outcome to deliverables, time to value and credible proof. Compare the proposed offer with the current alternative.

## Deliverable

Produce an offer card covering promise, scope, price assumptions and next step.

## Review

Verify that every promised deliverable can be fulfilled.
