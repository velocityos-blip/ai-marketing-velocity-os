# Buyer psychology

## Method

Map a buying obstacle to uncertainty, effort or perceived risk. Choose one honest way to reduce it.

## Deliverable

Draft a message variation and a hypothesis about buyer behavior.

## Review

Avoid invented urgency, testimonials and scarcity.
