# Search content quality review

## Method

Read supplied copy as a visitor with a specific question. Mark missing answers, unsupported claims, authorship signals and examples that demonstrate experience.

## Deliverable

A passage-level revision plan with replacement examples and evidence requests.

## Review

Distinguish quality recommendations from claims about ranking factors.
