# Demand generation

## Method

Start from a pipeline or revenue goal and audience. Map the buying group, demand capture and demand creation motions, campaign sequence, channels and handoffs. Estimate funnel requirements only when baseline rates are supplied; otherwise label assumptions.

## Deliverable

A campaign plan with audience, offer, asset sequence, channels, budget ceiling, owner, leading indicators, pipeline event and review date.

## Review

Do not equate leads with pipeline. State attribution limits, define qualified conversion criteria and include a stop or reallocation rule.
