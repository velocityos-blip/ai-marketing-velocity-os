# AI search visibility

## Method

List buyer questions and compare supplied answer samples for brand mentions, cited pages, and whether the cited content supports the answer. Record the engine, prompt, locale, date, and visible product/version. Repeat the same sample before drawing conclusions; generated answers and citations vary.

Use the focused workflows when the task needs deeper work: [AEO content briefs](../aeo-content-brief/SKILL.md), [AEO page edits](../aeo-page-optimization/SKILL.md), [crawler readiness](../aeo-crawler-readiness/SKILL.md), [GEO visibility audits](../geo-visibility-audit/SKILL.md), or [GEO measurement](../geo-measurement/SKILL.md).

## Deliverable

Return a question-to-source map with prompt, engine, date, locale, brand outcome, cited URLs, support quality, and the highest-priority answerable gaps.

## Review

Keep observations separate from recommendations. Avoid inferring visibility from a single answer or claiming that a content change guarantees inclusion.
