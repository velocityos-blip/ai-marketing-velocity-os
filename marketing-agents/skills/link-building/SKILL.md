# Link acquisition

## Method

Identify relevant publishers and the editorial reason each might cite a resource. Prioritize relevance over raw authority.

## Deliverable

Deliver a prospect rubric and a tailored pitch angle.

## Review

Exclude paid or deceptive links disguised as editorial coverage.
