# Strategy review

## Method

Review a proposed campaign from buyer, finance and distribution perspectives. Identify the disagreement that could change the decision.

## Deliverable

Recommend one course of action and a low-cost way to resolve the disagreement.

## Review

Explain which new evidence would reverse the recommendation.
