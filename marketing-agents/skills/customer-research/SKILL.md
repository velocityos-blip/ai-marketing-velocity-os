# Customer research

## Method

Segment customers by behavior. Ask about a recent buying decision and extract recurring problems from supplied observations.

## Deliverable

Deliver an interview guide and an evidence table linking claims to observations.

## Review

Keep verbatim feedback distinct from interpretation and proposed questions.
